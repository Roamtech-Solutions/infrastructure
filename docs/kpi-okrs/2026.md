# KPIs & OKRs
Key Performance Indicators & Objectives and Key Results 2026.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the key performance indicators (KPIs) and objectives that guide the Infrastructure organization in 2026.
These metrics establish clear expectations for reliability, security, scalability, and operational efficiency of the platform that supports all engineering teams and customer-facing services.

As Head of Infrastructure, the goal is to ensure that our systems remain highly available, secure, and performant while enabling rapid product development and sustainable growth.
The KPIs outlined here align infrastructure investments with business priorities, drive accountability across teams, and provide measurable indicators of progress in areas such as cloud adoption, automation, cost management, and operational excellence.

This framework ensures that infrastructure is not only stable and secure, but also a strategic enabler for product delivery and company growth.
